Changes: conversion to separately named glTF nodes, reconstruction of source
hierarchy, unit/axis conversion, landmark/extent registration into this atlas,
illustrative colours, grouping, new authored courses, Draco compression.
HRA renal pelvis and parenchyma share one placement transform. Ear registration
uses four anatomical landmarks. Different donors and edited placements are
combined for education; this is not one measured subject. Fourteen small nerve
courses are new illustrative reconstructions and labelled as such in the UI.
All 2,827 original selectable IDs are preserved, including two unidentified
legacy objects; unidentified objects are not given invented anatomical names.
See the per-part provenance and geometry notes for the exact scope.
